# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Cedarline Markets accepts returns and issues refunds across all stores and the online channel. Store associates, the online support team and the finance team all work from this document. Where a regional practice differs, this policy wins.

## Standard return window

Most merchandise can be returned within 30 days of purchase with a receipt or an order number. The day of purchase counts as day zero. Items must be unused and in their original packaging unless they are defective.

Gold loyalty members get an extended return window of 60 days on all categories except Electronics and Grocery. Silver members get 45 days. Bronze members follow the standard 30-day window.

## Category exceptions

### Electronics

Electronics have a 15-day return window for every customer, regardless of loyalty tier. Opened electronics are accepted only if all accessories, cables and manuals are included. Opened electronics that are not defective are subject to a 10% restocking fee, except for Gold members, who are exempt from the fee. Defective electronics are handled under the Electronics Warranty Policy after day 15.

### Grocery

Grocery items are not returnable. Perishable items with a quality problem (spoiled, damaged or past the printed date at the time of sale) are refunded or replaced on the spot without a receipt, up to 50 USD per visit. Larger claims go to the store manager.

### Beauty

Opened beauty products can be returned within 30 days only if the customer reports a skin reaction or the product is defective. Unopened beauty products follow the standard window.

### Apparel

Apparel follows the standard window. Swimwear and undergarments must have hygiene seals intact. Items marked final sale cannot be returned.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card and digital wallet refunds post within 5 to 7 business days. Cash purchases are refunded in cash up to 200 USD; above that, the refund is issued as a store credit or by check from the finance team within 10 business days.

Returns without a receipt are refunded as store credit at the lowest selling price in the last 60 days, limited to three receipt-less returns per customer per calendar year.

## Online orders

Online orders can be returned by mail using the prepaid label in the order confirmation, or in any store. Return shipping is free for defective items and for Gold members. Everyone else pays a flat 6.99 USD return shipping fee, deducted from the refund. Online returns are refunded within 3 business days of the item reaching the returns center.

## Status in reporting

When a return is processed, the original order line is marked RETURNED. Returned lines are excluded from net revenue and reported separately as returns amount. Cancelled orders (cancelled before fulfilment) are marked CANCELLED and are excluded from both revenue and returns.

## Fraud controls

Store managers must approve any single return above 500 USD. The loss prevention team reviews customers with more than 8 returns in a rolling 90-day period. A return rate above 12% for any single product over a quarter triggers a quality review with the merchandising team.
