# Online Ordering and Fulfillment Standards

## Channels

Online orders come from the website and the mobile app and are recorded with channel ONLINE. Customers pay by card or digital wallet. Cash is not accepted online.

## Fulfillment options

Customers choose ship-to-home or buy online, pick up in store. Pick-up orders are fulfilled by the selected store and must be ready within 2 hours during opening hours. Ship-to-home orders ship from the nearest store with stock or from the regional distribution center.

## Shipping fees

Standard shipping costs 5.99 USD and takes 3 to 5 business days. Orders over 50 USD ship free for all customers. Silver members get free standard shipping over 35 USD, and Gold members always ship free. Express shipping costs 12.99 USD and arrives in 1 to 2 business days.

## Service levels

95% of ship-to-home orders must leave the building within 24 hours. Customer support answers chat within 2 minutes and email within 12 hours.

## Cancellations

Customers can cancel an online order until it is picked. Cancelled orders are recorded with status CANCELLED and are excluded from revenue and returns.

## Substitutions

For Grocery orders, customers choose whether to allow substitutions. A substitute must be of equal or higher value and is charged at the price of the original item.
