# Supplier and Vendor Standards

## Scope

These standards apply to every supplier that sells goods to Cedarline Markets for resale, across all five categories.

## Delivery performance

Suppliers must deliver complete and on time. The on-time in-full (OTIF) target is 95% measured monthly. Below 90% for two consecutive months triggers a performance improvement plan. Below 85% allows Cedarline to apply a 3% chargeback on the invoice value of late or short shipments.

Grocery suppliers delivering perishables must deliver within the agreed two-hour window and with at least 70% of shelf life remaining.

## Quality

Suppliers are notified when a product's return rate passes 12% in a quarter (8% for Electronics). They must provide a root cause analysis within 15 business days and a corrective action plan within 30 days. Products that stay above the threshold for two quarters can be delisted.

## Cost and payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 45 days from receipt of goods. Suppliers can offer a 2% discount for payment within 10 days. Cost price changes need 60 days' written notice, except for Grocery commodities, which need 14 days.

## Promotional funding

Suppliers may fund promotions. Supplier-funded promotions still follow the category discount maximums in the Pricing and Promotions Playbook.

## Responsible sourcing

All suppliers must sign the Supplier Code of Conduct, covering labour standards, safety, environmental practices and anti-corruption. Cedarline audits high-risk suppliers at least every two years.
