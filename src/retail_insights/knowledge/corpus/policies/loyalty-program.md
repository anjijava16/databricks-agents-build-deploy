# Cedarline Rewards Loyalty Program Terms

## Overview

Cedarline Rewards is the free loyalty program for Cedarline Markets customers. Members earn points on purchases in stores and online and move between three tiers: Bronze, Silver and Gold. Guest checkouts do not earn points and are not linked to a customer profile.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point per 1 USD of net spend after discounts. Silver members earn 1.25 points per dollar and Gold members earn 1.5 points per dollar. Points are not earned on gift card purchases, taxes, shipping fees or returned items. When an order line is returned, the points earned on it are removed.

## Tiers

### Bronze

Every new member starts at Bronze. Bronze members receive member-only weekly offers and birthday rewards.

### Silver

Members reach Silver after 750 USD of net spend in a calendar year. Silver benefits include a 45-day return window, free standard shipping on online orders over 35 USD and early access to seasonal sales.

### Gold

Members reach Gold after 2,000 USD of net spend in a calendar year. Gold benefits include a 60-day return window on eligible categories, free shipping on every online order, free return shipping, exemption from the electronics restocking fee and a quarterly 10% off coupon for Home and Apparel.

Tier status lasts for the rest of the year in which it was earned and the whole of the following calendar year.

## Redeeming points

Every 500 points can be redeemed for a 5 USD reward. Rewards can be used in stores and online and expire 90 days after they are issued. Points themselves expire after 18 months without any qualifying purchase.

## Stacking rules

Loyalty rewards can be combined with one promotional discount per order line. The combined discount from promotions on a single line must still respect the limits in the Pricing and Promotions Playbook. Loyalty rewards are applied after promotional discounts.

## Data use

Purchase history is used to personalise offers and to segment customers with the RFM model described in the Customer Segmentation Playbook. Members can opt out of personalised offers at any time without losing points.
