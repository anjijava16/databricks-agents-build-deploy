# Electronics Warranty Policy

## Coverage

All Electronics sold by Cedarline Markets carry a 12-month store warranty against manufacturing defects, starting on the purchase date. The warranty covers repair or replacement with the same or an equivalent model. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, lost accessories or normal wear of batteries after 6 months.

## How warranty claims work

Within the first 15 days, a defective electronics item is handled as a return under the Returns and Refunds Policy. From day 16 to the end of month 12, customers bring the item and proof of purchase to any store or start a claim online. The store tests the item; if the defect is confirmed, the customer receives a replacement from stock or a repair voucher. If no equivalent model is available, the customer receives store credit for the original net price.

## Extended protection plan

Customers can buy a Cedarline Protect plan at checkout for items over 50 USD. Plans cost 15% of the item price for 2 years or 22% for 3 years and add accidental damage cover with one replacement per year. Protection plans are reported separately and are not part of product net revenue.

## Product quality monitoring

The merchandising team reviews warranty claims monthly. Any Electronics product with a return rate above 8% in a quarter, or more than 20 warranty claims in a month, is escalated to the supplier under the Supplier and Vendor Standards.
