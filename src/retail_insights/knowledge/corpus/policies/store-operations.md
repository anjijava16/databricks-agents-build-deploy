# Store Operations Handbook

## Network

Cedarline Markets operates nine stores across four regions. Northeast: Philadelphia, New York and Boston. South: Atlanta and Austin. Midwest: Chicago and Columbus. West: Seattle and Denver. Each store is led by a store manager who reports to a regional director.

## Opening hours

Stores open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM local time, seven days a week. Holiday hours are published four weeks in advance.

## Cash handling

Registers are counted at every shift change. A cash variance above 20 USD on a single register must be reported to the store manager the same day, and above 100 USD to the regional loss prevention lead.

## Inventory and shrink

Stores run cycle counts every week for Electronics and Beauty, which have the highest shrink risk, and every month for other categories. The shrink target is below 1.5% of net revenue per store per quarter. Stores above 2% are put on a shrink action plan.

## Weekly performance review

Every Monday, store managers review the previous week's net revenue, gross margin, average order value, returns amount and units by channel against the same week last year. The regional director reviews the bottom two stores in each region by gross margin.

## Staffing

Weekend traffic is typically 30 to 40 percent higher than weekdays, so weekend rotas schedule more cashiers and floor staff. Electronics departments must have at least one trained associate on the floor during opening hours.
